# Oracle-injection figure suite — run_20260612_200000

Figures generated by `modular_addition/oracle/experiments/plot_exp*.py` (shared
conventions in `plot_common.py`). Regenerate any experiment with:

```
.venv/bin/python modular_addition/oracle/experiments/plot_<exp>.py \
    modular_addition/oracle/results/run_20260612_200000
```

**Conventions (all figures):** 4 seeds drawn faint + bold seed-mean; the
continuous sweep axis (n / amp / rel) is viridis, baseline/n=0 is gray;
never-grokked runs are never silently averaged (filtered with `only_groked`, or
shown explicitly as censored markers / success-rate heatmaps); a one-line
caption is burned into the bottom of every PNG. **M** = main-hypothesis figure,
**S** = sanity-check / confound guard.

All findings below were spot-checked against the raw `result.json` data, not
just read off the plots. Three experiments **contradicted their stated
hypotheses** — flagged ⚠️. Those are real (verified), not plotting artifacts.

## exp01 — uptake & completeness (p=113), 15 figures ✅ confirms hypothesis
**Hypothesis:** the model uses the injected frequencies; a small complete basis
suffices to grok. **Verdict:** confirmed, with a two-threshold refinement.

**Finding:** injected freqs are taken up into W_E (60–95% of spectral power) and
are causally used. Sharp completeness **floor at n=3** (n≥3 groks ~400 ep vs
~10k baseline); **n=1–2 is a "valley of death"** (several seeds never grok).
Independence is bought by *redundancy*, not amplitude: ablating the live oracle
collapses n=3 (acc_off≈0.4) but barely dents n=8 (≈0.9).

- M `acc_overlay` / `acc_subplots` — test acc vs epoch by n; grok dynamics.
- M `grok_vs_n` — epochs-to-grok vs n; threshold at 3, censored failures.
- M `uptake_overlay` / `uptake_subplots` — W_E power fraction on injected freqs.
- M `injected_in_key_overlay` — injected freqs retained as key freqs (100%→62%).
- M `we_spectrum` — final W_E spectrum per regime (recruitment below threshold).
- S `ablation_acc_*` / `ablation_delta_*` / `ablation_final` — oracle-off causal dependence.
- S `trig_excluded_*` — sufficiency (trig) vs necessity (excluded) per n.
- S `sanity_excluded_gini` — per-freq necessity + W_E spectral concentration.

## exp02_1 — delayed injection (T∈{4000,8000}), 5 figures ⚠️ contradicts hypothesis
**Hypothesis:** the oracle drives grokking, so injecting at epoch T should make
the model adopt the injected freqs and grok shortly after T. **Verdict:
contradicted — there is a critical period.**

**Finding (verified):** a late-arriving oracle is **completely ignored**.
Injection gating is correct (`injecting` flips True at T), yet grok timing is
identical to the **n=0 baseline** regardless of n or T (n=3 inject@4000:
[9.6k,10k,4.6k,7.2k] vs baseline [10k,11k,4.6k,8k]; even n=8 inject@8000 groks
on baseline schedule, vs ~400 ep when injected from epoch 0 in exp01). Uptake
collapses to ~0.01–0.13 (vs 0.90–0.96 in exp01) and the model stays
oracle-**independent** (acc_off≈0.75–1.0). Injection only shapes the circuit if
present from the start.

- M `1_acc_vs_epoch_by_T` — grok onset is at baseline absolute epoch, not T+lag.
- M `2_uptake_vs_epoch_by_T` — no uptake jump at T; embedding ignores the oracle.
- M `5_grok_lag_vs_T` — grok lag (grok−T) is *not* shared across T ⇒ grok is time-driven, not injection-driven (the money figure).
- S `3_event_aligned_uptake` — T-curves don't collapse when re-zeroed to injection.
- S `4_ablation_accoff_vs_epoch_by_T` — model is fully functional with oracle off.

## exp02_2 — amplitude (0.5–4×), 7 figures ◐ partial
**Hypothesis:** higher oracle amplitude makes W_E lazy (offload to oracle) and
can destabilize training. **Verdict:** dependence-laziness confirmed; structural
laziness partial; destabilization confirmed but driven by *low n*, not amp alone.

**Finding:** behavioural dependence rises cleanly with amp (acc_off n=3: 0.97→0.11).
18/96 runs fail to grok, **entirely at n∈{1,2}**, amp-modulated and
non-monotonic (amp=2 kills n=1,2 entirely). Structural laziness (lower |W_E|) is
clear only for n≥3.

- M `dependence_accoff_vs_amp` — amplitude-robust dependence metric (rises with amp).
- M `laziness_we_norm_vs_amp` — |W_E| drops with amp for n≥3 (offloading).
- M `laziness_uptake_vs_amp` — self-power on injected freqs vs amp.
- S `grok_success_heatmap` / `grok_epoch_heatmap` — failure cluster guard (amp×n).
- S `amp_scaling_caveat` — raw ΔCE rises with amp (mechanical confound; use acc_off).
- S `perf_vs_amp` — conditioned on grok, high amp doesn't cost final accuracy.

## exp04 — oracle reliability (1.0→0.0), 6 figures ⚠️ contradicts hypothesis
**Hypothesis:** higher n (more pairs) gives robustness to corruption.
**Verdict: contradicted — high n does not rescue low reliability.**

**Finding (verified):** reliability acts as a binary grok/no-grok gate between
rel=0.5 and 0.25, not a graded dependence slide. At rel=0.25, high n (5,6,8) all
collapse to **0/4** grok while n=1 still gets 2/4 — redundancy hurts, not helps,
under heavy corruption. n=2 is a persistent dead zone (the exp01 valley again).
Among grokked runs, acc_off≈1.0: a model that groks under corruption has
internalized the solution and barely uses the live oracle. ⚠️ corruption draw is
seeded by model seed, so per-seed spread mixes init and corruption randomness.

- M `grok_success_heatmap` / `grok_epoch_heatmap` — the reliability threshold (headline).
- M `dependence_vs_rel` — grokked models are oracle-independent across rel.
- M `uptake_vs_rel` — uptake of the true freqs vs reliability.
- S `injected_in_key_vs_rel` — true freqs retained in working set vs rel.
- S `acc_curves_by_rel` — all runs incl failures, exposing the never-grok cluster.

## exp05 — answer-hint oracle, 6 figures ⚠️ contradicts hypothesis
**Hypothesis:** a weak answer hint accelerates learning and simplifies the basis.
**Verdict: contradicted on acceleration.** NB: the hint leaks the label, so final
accuracy is trivial — figures use accuracy only for *timing*.

**Finding (verified):** every hint config groks *later* than baseline (~8.4k);
onehot configs ~2× slower (~16–17.5k), fourier closest (~10k). Only the *fourier*
hint simplifies (≈2.75 key freqs vs baseline 5.0) and shows the strongest
dependence (acc_off≈0.12); onehot hints mostly just slow grokking. The
laziness/simplification/dependence signals all line up on the *weak* (fourier)
hint, not the strong (onehot) one.

- M `acceleration` / `grok_epoch_by_config` — timing vs baseline (hints slow it).
- M `dependence_accoff` — crutch test (baseline excluded; fourier most dependent).
- M `laziness_nkeyfreqs` — solution simplification (fourier only).
- S `laziness_we` — W_E norm + Gini by config.
- S `spectrum_compare` — hint changes solution *shape*, not just speed.

## exp06 — exp01's analyses rebuilt on richer data (p=211, frac=0.075, 75k), 6 figures
exp06 strictly dominates exp01 as a data source (contiguous n=0..11, larger
model, low-data rescue regime). This **curated, subplot-only set replaces the
exp01 figures** for these questions; it tests the same things on better data.
All six are per-n subplot grids or vs-n dashboards — no overlays.

**Findings (verified):** (1) **completeness/rescue** — low-data n=0,1,2 never
grok; injection rescues sharply at n=3. (2) **uptake** — n≥3 concentrate 70–95%
of W_E power on the injected freqs. (3) **spectrum** — power lands exactly on
injected sites; n=2 stays diffuse. (4,6) **ablation reverses the p=113 trend** —
dependence on the live oracle (ON−OFF gap, CE delta) *grows* with n here, where
at p=113 larger bases became ablation-proof. (5) **sufficiency** — injected-only
trig loss collapses toward full loss for n≥3. (6) **decisive: the kept-set
ceiling MOVES** — working set climbs past the p=113 ~5 line to ~9–10 by n=11,
supporting the capacity-bound (parameter-cost) hypothesis.

- M `grok_dynamics` — test acc per n (0..11); the rescue threshold at n=3.
- M `uptake` — W_E power fraction on injected freqs per n.
- M `spectrum` — final W_E Fourier spectrum (105 freqs) per n, injected marked.
- S `ablation` — accuracy oracle ON vs OFF per n (causal use; gap grows with n).
- S `sufficiency` — trig loss (injected-only vs key-freqs vs full) per n.
- M `summary_vs_n` — 2×2 dashboard: (a) kept-set ceiling + p=113 line [decisive], (b) retained fraction, (c) acc ON vs OFF, (d) CE delta — all vs n.

⚠️ **Data caveat:** `excluded_loss_injected` (exp01's necessity metric) is 100%
NaN at p=211 — `calculate_excluded_loss` blows up numerically at the larger
prime. Necessity is therefore carried by the ablation figure (causal removal of
the live signal). Worth fixing upstream if you want the excluded-loss view.

### Cross-experiment threads
- **Valley of death at n=1–2** recurs in exp01, exp02_2 (grok failures), and exp04 (n=2 dead zone) — a partial/incomplete basis is worse than none.
- **Critical period (exp02_1)** + **scaling ceiling (exp06)** are the two cleanest new results; the ceiling figure decides the parameter-cost hypothesis in its favor.
- **Dependence reverses with scale:** at p=113 (exp01) larger bases became ablation-proof (independent); at p=211 low-data (exp06) dependence on the live oracle *grows* with n. Worth understanding before generalizing exp01's independence story.
- **acc_off is the robust dependence metric**; raw ablation ΔCE is amplitude-confounded (see exp02_2 `amp_scaling_caveat`).
